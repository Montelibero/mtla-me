Our Bodies

Assembly

The highest governing body of the Association, making decisions based on liquid democracy. The Assembly clarifies goals, approves strategic plans, and addresses key issues within the Association's framework.

Learn more about the Assembly →

Council

The executive body ensuring implementation of the Assembly's decisions. The Council handles budgeting, accounting, blockchain multi-signature, and serves as the default guarantor and arbitrator in the Montelibero legal system.

Learn more about the Council →

MTL Court

An arbitration court. An institution created to provide an optimal mechanism for resolving disputes between participants and supporters of the movement.

Learn more about MTL Court →

Distributed Management

A coordination platform for activists and experts, occupying the place of an "extended government" in the Montelibero Association. Designed to ensure optimal configuration of coordinated management of common tasks.

Learn more about DM →

Mission Control Center

A coordination platform for MTLA officials and coordinators of key projects, programs, and areas of activity.

Learn more about MCC →

Working Groups

Bodies for preliminary elaboration of issues submitted to the Council. They perform the role of "parliamentary committees".

Learn more about WG →
